
# GoalPath AI Wealth

AI-powered mutual fund and bonds marketplace for goal-based investing and income enhancement.

## Introduction

### Product Vision
Empower individuals to achieve financial goals through an optimized platform that uses AI for personalized mutual fund and bond selection, alongside enhanced passive income strategies. 

### Problem Statement
Investors struggle with complex markets, misaligned goals, and suboptimal decisions. Existing platforms lack personalized, goal-oriented, and intelligent optimization, leading to missed income opportunities and reduced financial clarity.

### Solution Overview
AI-driven marketplace simplifying goal-based investing. Uses advanced algorithms to deliver personalized investment recommendations and passive income strategies for users, minimizing active effort.

### Business Goals

- Simplify goal-based investing for retail investors.
- Improve investment outcomes using AI optimization.
- Increase platform engagement and retention.
- Generate revenue through fees and premium features.

## Target Audience

- Goal-oriented investors (e.g., home purchase, child's education, retirement).
- Beginners to intermediates needing guidance.
- Time-sensitive professionals.
- Users seeking passive income from financial assets.
- Young professionals, working parents, pre-retirees.

## Key Features

### Core Investment Marketplace

- Discover and research mutual funds and bonds (equity, debt, hybrid, thematic).
- View detailed fund/bond profiles: performance, expense ratios, risk and credit ratings.
- Filter & search investments by criteria (asset class, risk, fund house, maturity).
- Secure investment process.

### AI-Powered Goal-Based Investment Engine

- Define financial goals (e.g., retirement, education fund, target returns).
- Risk profiling and tailored recommendations.
- Portfolio suggestions and goal-tracking dashboard.
- Portfolio rebalancing and AI explanations.

### Income Enhancement Optimization

- Automated dividend/interest reinvestment.
- Suggestions for high-yield savings, income streams, and bond ladders.
- Clear effort-impact assessment for income strategies.

### Portfolio Management & Reporting

- Consolidated investment views.
- Performance tracking & downloadable reports.
- SIP & SWP management.

### Educational Resources & Tools

- Articles, videos, tutorials on investment concepts.
- Calculators (SIP, goal planner).
- Risk education.

## Technical Requirements (High-Level)

- Real-time & batch data ingestion (market data, user profiles, NAVs, bond prices).
- ML-powered recommendation and risk engines (Monte Carlo, deep learning).
- Scalability via cloud-native architectures (AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Secure data & regulatory compliance (encryption, MFA, GDPR/SEBI/FINRA).
- Well-documented APIs & integration with KYC, payment, bank systems.

## Success Metrics

- Assets Under Management (AUM).
- User acquisition and goal achievement rates.
- Platform engagement and retention.
- AI feature adoption and customer satisfaction (NPS).
- Revenue and portfolio performance growth.
